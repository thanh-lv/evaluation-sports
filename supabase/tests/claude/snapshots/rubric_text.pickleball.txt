# Rubric: pickleball (pb-2026.10.18+drills-2026.10.18)

## Sub-scores

- stance: ready position, base width, knee bend, balance
- footwork: split step, steps and lunges into the hitting position
- swing: body turn, backswing, elbow and arm path
- contact: contact height, position in front of the body, arm extension
- follow: follow-through direction and finish
- recovery: returning to a balanced ready position after the stroke

## Drills

- pb.drill.ready_hold | Ready-position hold | 3 sets × 30 s | Stand with feet shoulder-width or wider, knees bent, weight slightly forward.
- pb.drill.paddle_up_reaction | Paddle-up reaction volleys | 3 sets × 15 reps | Stand two to three meters from a wall, paddle in front of your chest.
- pb.drill.split_on_cue | Split step on a cue | 3 sets × 10 reps | Stand in the ready position, paddle in front.
- pb.drill.pendulum_swing | Slow pendulum swings | 3 sets × 10 reps | Stand with your non-paddle foot forward and your weight on the back foot.
- pb.drill.deep_serve_target | Deep serves to a target | 3 sets × 10 reps | Place a towel about one meter inside the far baseline.
- pb.drill.no_backswing_dink | No-backswing dinks | 3 sets × 20 reps | Stand at the kitchen line with your knees bent.
- pb.drill.knee_bend_dink | Knee-bend dinks | 3 sets × 60 s | Dink cross-court with a partner or against a wall.
- pb.drill.shuffle_dink | Shuffle dinks | 3 sets × 60 s | Stand at the kitchen line facing the net.
- pb.drill.dink_rally | Dink rally | 3 sets × 30 reps | Dink back and forth with a partner, aiming for thirty in a row.
- pb.drill.drop_ladder | Drop ladder | 3 sets × 10 reps | Start at mid-court and hit soft drops into the kitchen.
- pb.drill.drop_and_split | Drop, move up and split | 3 sets × 8 reps | Get settled at the baseline before you hit the drop.
- pb.drill.return_and_run | Return and run to the kitchen | 3 sets × 10 reps | Have a partner serve; alone, drop the ball yourself.
- pb.drill.unit_turn_shadow | Unit-turn shadow swings | 3 sets × 10 reps | Start in the ready position.
- pb.drill.brush_up | Low-to-high brush | 3 sets × 10 reps | Shadow swing from low to high, as if brushing up the back of the ball.
- pb.drill.wall_punch_volley | Wall punch volleys | 3 sets × 30 reps | Stand about two meters from a wall, paddle in front of your chest.
- pb.drill.turn_and_step_back | Turn and crossover back | 3 sets × 6 reps | Stand at the kitchen line in the ready position.
