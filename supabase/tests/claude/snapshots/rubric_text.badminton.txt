# Rubric: badminton (bd-2026.10.18+drills-2026.10.18)

## Sub-scores

- stance: ready position, base width, knee bend, balance
- footwork: split step, steps and lunges into the hitting position
- swing: body turn, backswing, elbow and arm path
- contact: contact height, position in front of the body, arm extension
- follow: follow-through direction and finish
- recovery: returning to a balanced ready position after the stroke

## Drills

- bd.drill.ready_hold | Ready-stance hold | 3 sets × 30 s | Stand with feet a bit wider than your shoulders, knees soft, chest slightly forward.
- bd.drill.racket_up_bounce | Racket-up shuttle bounces | 3 sets × 20 reps | Stand in the ready stance, racket in front of your chest.
- bd.drill.split_on_cue | Split step on a cue | 3 sets × 10 reps | Stand in the ready stance.
- bd.drill.shadow_turn_point | Shadow turn-and-point | 3 sets × 10 reps | Start in the ready stance.
- bd.drill.point_pull | Point and pull | 3 sets × 10 reps | Raise the free hand and point up.
- bd.drill.towel_snap | Towel snap | 3 sets × 10 reps | Hold one end of a towel like a racket.
- bd.drill.overhead_throw | Overhead shuttle throw | 3 sets × 15 reps | Stand side-on, racket-side foot back.
- bd.drill.hanging_shuttle | Shadow swings at a hanging shuttle | 3 sets × 15 reps | Hang a shuttle on a string at the height your racket reaches with your arm fully up.
- bd.drill.scissor_shadow | Rear-court shadow with scissor kick | 3 sets × 8 reps | Start in the middle of the court in the ready stance.
- bd.drill.opposite_hip_finish | Finish at the opposite hip | 3 sets × 10 reps | Shadow a clear swing.
- bd.drill.hit_and_recover | Hit and recover to base | 3 sets × 8 reps | Play a clear from the back court, real or shadow.
- bd.drill.kneeling_smash | Kneeling smash swings | 3 sets × 10 reps | Kneel on one knee on a soft mat, non-racket knee down.
- bd.drill.jump_land_hold | Jump, land and hold | 3 sets × 8 reps | Stand with feet shoulder-width apart.
- bd.drill.smash_step_in | Smash and step in | 3 sets × 8 reps | From mid-court, shadow a smash with contact in front of your forehead.
- bd.drill.mirror_serve | Mirror serves | 3 sets × 10 reps | Stand side-on to a mirror, or film yourself with your phone.
- bd.drill.serve_string | Serve under a string | 3 sets × 10 reps | Stretch a string about a hand-span above the net tape.
- bd.drill.serve_racket_up | Serve, then racket up | 3 sets × 10 reps | Make one low serve.
- bd.drill.net_touch | Net shots from the contact position | 3 sets × 15 reps | Stand near the net with the racket already at the contact point, at tape height.
- bd.drill.lunge_push_back | Slow lunge and push back | 3 sets × 8 reps | From mid-court, lunge with your racket leg toward a front corner.
- bd.drill.lift_target | Lifts to a back-court target | 3 sets × 10 reps | Place a target, such as a towel, near the back line.
- bd.drill.high_serve_freeze | High serve, finish and freeze | 3 sets × 10 reps | Stand side-on, non-racket foot forward, weight on the back foot.
