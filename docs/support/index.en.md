## Filming

### How do I film a clip that analyzes well?

Quick version:
1. Landscape, phone fixed in place (tripod, fence clamp, shelf on the back wall), 1× lens.
2. **Badminton:** put the phone behind the court and up high, or at the back-corner diagonal. **Pickleball:** on the side fence, level with where you hit, 3–5 m away.
3. Whole body and racket in frame; the player fills about ½–⅔ of the frame height.
4. Record at 60 fps. With the Camera app: Settings › Camera › Record Video › 1080p HD at 60 fps, and turn off "Auto FPS".
5. Even light, no backlight.
6. One stroke, repeated 5–10 times, at most 30 seconds.

The full guide is in the app (Home › Camera guide) and at https://{{SUPPORT_HOST}}/en/camera-guide.

### The hall is crowded and I can't film from the side. What now?

Use the behind or diagonal view. You still get an analysis; the app marks the points that view cannot measure.

### Can I use a video a friend sent me on Zalo?

Yes. Open the video and choose **Share › RacketCoach**, or save it to Photos or Files and pick it in the app. Messaging apps usually compress video; if possible, ask for the original sent as a file.

### Can I analyze a video of someone else?

Yes, if they agreed. The app asks you to confirm this. If the person is under 16, you must be their parent or guardian, or have that person's consent; the app then sends pose data only and no images.

## When a clip is rejected

### Why was my clip rejected?

The app checks every clip before submitting it. A clip rejected at this stage **does not use a credit**. Common reasons:

| The app says | What to do |
|---|---|
| No strokes detected | Repeat one stroke 5–10 times; whole body in frame; record at 60 fps |
| Player too small | Move the phone closer |
| Player hard to see | Add light, avoid backlight, keep the phone still |
| Clip too short or too long | Keep the clip between 3 and 30 seconds |
| Lost track of the player | Keep only one person in frame |
| Frame rate too low | Record again at 60 fps with "Auto FPS" off |
| Wrong sport or skill | Pick the right skill before recording |

### What does "Not enough data — no credit used" mean?

The system did not have enough reliable data to score the stroke, usually because of the camera angle or light. The credit is refunded (up to 3 times per period). Film again using the tips on screen.

### My analysis failed halfway.

Keep the app open while it processes. If a system error caused the failure, the credit is always refunded. If the app says the service is busy, try again in a few minutes.

- The current limits are always shown on the Premium screen.

### What's the difference between an advanced and a basic analysis?

An advanced analysis uses a more capable AI model (Claude Sonnet) for more detailed feedback. A basic analysis uses a lighter model (Claude Haiku). Every number and score is measured by the same system in both.

### How do I cancel?

Go to **iPhone Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions › RacketCoach Premium › Cancel Subscription**, or in the app: **Settings › Subscription › Manage**. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Premium stays active until the end of the paid period. Deleting the app or your account does **not** cancel the subscription.

### How do I restore my purchase?

In the app, go to **Settings › Subscription › Restore Purchases** (or tap Restore on the Premium screen). Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

If the app says the subscription is linked to another account and asks you to tap Restore, do so: the subscription moves to the account you are using now. A subscription can move at most 3 times in 30 days.

### How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments. Request a refund at https://reportaproblem.apple.com. If you turned on "share usage information with Apple on refund requests", we send Apple a summary of your usage to help it decide; otherwise we send nothing.

### Where do I enter a promo code?

**Settings › Subscription › Redeem Code**, or the Redeem button on the Premium screen.

## Account and devices

### Do I need an account?

No. The app creates an anonymous account for you. To keep your history when you change phones, choose **Save history & use on other devices** and sign in with Apple or email.

### How do I keep my history on a new iPhone?

Before switching, link your account (Sign in with Apple or email). On the new phone, sign in the same way. Restore Premium with Restore Purchases. Original videos stay on the old phone and are not transferred.

### How do I delete my account?

Go to **Settings › Delete account** and confirm. This works even if you never signed in. All history, pose data, profile and consent choices are permanently deleted. It does not cancel Premium; cancel the subscription as described above.

### How do I delete videos stored on my phone?

**Settings › Storage › Delete local videos**. The app also deletes videos older than 60 days.

## Privacy and AI

### What does the app send?

Your video is **never** uploaded. After you agree, the app sends pose data and up to 5 still images (faces blurred on a best-effort basis, other people masked) to our servers in Singapore and to Anthropic's Claude AI (USA). The images are deleted as soon as the analysis finishes. Details: **Settings › Privacy › What we send**, and the Privacy Policy at https://{{LEGAL_HOST}}/en/privacy.

### How do I withdraw consent?

**Settings › Privacy › Withdraw consent.** You then can't start new analyses until you agree again. To delete stored data, delete analyses in History or delete your account.

### How accurate are the scores? Why "beta"?

Scores are measured from 2D video from one camera, so they depend on the camera angle and light. They are rounded to 5 and still in beta. Use them to track your own progress on the same stroke from the same view. Feedback is AI-generated and may be inaccurate.

### Is this medical advice?

No. RacketCoach is a sports technique learning aid. If something hurts, stop and ask a coach or a professional.
