## 1. Who we are

RacketCoach is an iPhone app that helps badminton and pickleball players improve their technique with AI-generated feedback. It is developed and operated by **{{COMPANY_NAME}}** ("we"), {{COMPANY_ADDRESS}}, registration number {{COMPANY_REG_NO}}.

For the personal data described in this policy we act as the **personal data controller and processor**. You are the **data subject**. Privacy contact: **{{CONTACT_EMAIL}}**.

## 2. Summary

- **Your video is never uploaded.** The app analyzes it on your iPhone.
- For the analysis we send **pose data** (the positions of your body joints over time) and **up to 5 still images** cropped around the player. In those images, faces are blurred automatically on a best-effort basis (which cannot be guaranteed in every case) and other people are masked.
- Still images are **deleted as soon as the analysis finishes**, and at the latest after 72 hours.
- Data is stored in **Singapore** (Supabase) and analyzed by **Anthropic's Claude AI (USA)**. We only do this with your consent.
- No account needed. No ads, no tracking, no sale of data.
- You can withdraw consent, delete single analyses or delete your whole account in the app.
- The app is for people aged **18 and over**.

## 3. Data we process

### 3.2 Data that stays on your iPhone

- The original video and the trimmed clip. The app deletes videos older than 60 days (with a notice); you can delete them at any time in **Settings › Storage**. This folder is excluded from iCloud backup.
- Unblurred frames used to show your results on the device.
- A copy of your results for viewing offline.

### 3.3 Data we do not collect

Audio (the app does not use the microphone), your name, phone number, contacts, location, photo library (you pick individual videos through the iOS picker), and advertising identifiers. The app contains no third-party analytics or advertising tools.

Feedback is written by **Claude**, an AI model made by **Anthropic PBC** (USA). Our code computes the numbers (angles, scores); the AI judges points that can only be seen in images and writes the feedback. Feedback may be inaccurate and is not medical advice. Details: **AI Disclosure** at https://{{LEGAL_HOST}}/en/ai.

## 7. Transfers outside Vietnam

Your data is transferred outside Vietnam: to **Singapore** (Supabase) and the **USA** (Anthropic, Apple). We only transfer it after you consent to options (a) and (b), with these safeguards:
- we send only the minimum needed for the analysis; your video never leaves your iPhone;
- faces are blurred and other people masked before images leave your iPhone; no images of people under 16 are sent;
- encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest;
- data processing agreements with each recipient;
- images deleted right after the analysis, and limited retention for everything else (section 8).

We prepare and file the cross-border transfer impact assessment with the competent authority as the law requires.

## 9. Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) and data is encrypted at rest. Each user can access only their own data (row- and folder-level access rules). Apple sign-in tokens are encrypted separately. Technical logs contain no analysis content. Only a small number of operators can access the systems, on a least-privilege basis.

If a data breach happens, we will notify the competent authority and you within the time limits set by law.

## 11. Withdrawing consent

In **Settings › Privacy** you can:
- **Withdraw consent (a) or (b):** we stop accepting new analyses from you. Stored data remains until you delete the analysis or your account; to delete it as well, follow section 10 or section 12.
- **Turn off (c) accuracy calibration** or **(d) sharing with Apple on refund requests:** this takes effect for all later processing.

Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before.

## 12. Deleting your account

Go to **Settings › Delete account** (this also works if you never signed in). When you confirm, we:
1. revoke the app's Sign in with Apple access (if used);
2. delete all your images, pose data, results, profile, consent records, credits and usage events;
3. remove the link between you and your purchases;
4. keep only a one-way hashed audit record that is not used to identify you.

**Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription.** Cancel it in **iPhone Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions**. If the app cannot revoke Sign in with Apple itself, you can do it in **iPhone Settings › [your name] › Sign-In & Security › Sign in with Apple**.

## 13. Age

RacketCoach is for people **aged 18 and over**. The app asks for your birth year and does not let younger people use it. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we delete it.

**Filming children:** if the person in the clip is under 16, the user must be their parent or legal guardian, or have that person's consent. The analysis then uses pose data only and **no images are uploaded**.

## 14. Filming other people

You may only film and analyze someone else with their consent; the app asks you to confirm this before the analysis. The app automatically masks everyone except the player and blurs faces on a best-effort basis. A person who was filmed can contact {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} to ask us to delete data about them.

## 15. No tracking, no advertising

We do not track you across other apps or websites, do not use advertising identifiers, and do not share data with data brokers or ad networks.

## 16. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we update the effective date at the top. If a change affects what you consented to, the app asks for your consent again before your next analysis.
