## Description (≤ 4000)

```
RacketCoach helps beginner badminton and pickleball players see what to fix first, from one short clip filmed on an iPhone.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Pick the stroke you want to practice.
2. Set up your phone with the camera guide and film 5–10 reps of one stroke (up to 30 seconds), or choose an existing video.
3. Confirm the strokes and get your feedback in about a minute.

WHAT YOU GET
• "Fix this first": the most important thing to change, with a short cue, a drill and a key frame with a skeleton overlay.
• An overall score and 6 sub-scores (beta): stance & balance, footwork, swing mechanics, contact point, follow-through, recovery.
• Strengths and things to improve, each based on evidence measured from your clip.
• A practice plan with up to 3 priorities, each with specific drills.
• Progress tracking across your analyses (Premium).

SUPPORTED STROKES
Badminton: forehand overhead clear, smash, low backhand serve.
Pickleball: serve, dink, third-shot drop.

PRIVACY
• Your video is analyzed on your iPhone and never uploaded.
• After you agree, only pose data and up to 5 still images (faces automatically blurred on a best-effort basis) are sent for AI analysis. The images are deleted as soon as the analysis finishes.
• No account needed. No ads.

FREE AND PREMIUM
• Free: 2 analyses per month; your first one is an advanced analysis.
• Premium: 15 advanced analyses per month, full reports, video playback with skeleton, progress tracking, full history.
• The yearly plan includes a 7-day free trial for eligible users (up to 10 analyses, 5 of them advanced).

Premium is an auto-renewing monthly or yearly subscription. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase, or when the free trial ends. The subscription renews automatically unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; renewal is charged within 24 hours before the current period ends. Manage or cancel in iPhone Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions. Any unused part of a free trial is forfeited when you buy a subscription.

Terms of Use (EULA): https://{{LEGAL_HOST}}/en/terms
Privacy Policy: https://{{LEGAL_HOST}}/en/privacy

Feedback is AI-generated (Claude by Anthropic) and may be inaccurate. RacketCoach is a sports technique learning aid, not medical advice. For ages 18 and over.
```
