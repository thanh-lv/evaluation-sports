# App Review notes and submission checklist

Two parts: **(A)** the text pasted into App Store Connect › App Review Information › Notes (≤ 4000 characters, English), and **(B)** the operator checklist to run before every submission. Sources: DESIGN.md §2.2, §9, §11 (rows 2.1, 3.1.2, 5.1.1(v), 5.1.2(i)), `docs/database.md` (reviewer flag).

## A. Notes for App Review (paste between the fences)

```
RacketCoach gives beginner badminton and pickleball players AI feedback on one stroke filmed with the iPhone camera. The video is analyzed on the device with Apple Vision and is never uploaded; only pose data and up to 5 still images (faces blurred on a best-effort basis, other people masked) are sent after the user consents.

NO LOGIN NEEDED
The app signs in anonymously on first launch. Every feature, including purchases and account deletion, works without an account. Sign in with Apple or email is optional ("Save history & use on other devices", shown after the first result).

QUICK TEST (about 3 minutes)
1. Welcome screen: tap "See a sample analysis" for an offline demo result, or "Get started".
   The app defaults to Vietnamese on the Vietnam storefront. To switch: Settings > Language > English.
2. About you: enter a birth year that makes you 18 or older (e.g. 1990). Under-18 is blocked by design.
3. Home > "Camera guide & sample clip" > "Analyze the sample clip". This runs the real pipeline on a bundled clip, so no badminton court is needed. The result arrives in under 90 seconds.
4. Before the first submission the app shows the AI consent screen. It names Anthropic (Claude, USA) and Supabase (Singapore), lists what is sent and how long it is kept. Items (a) and (b) are required; (c) and (d) are optional and unticked.
5. Results: the "fix this first" card, the beta score, strengths, and the label "AI-generated feedback, may be inaccurate · Not medical advice". Free users see part of the report; the rest is locked with a count.

SUBSCRIPTIONS (sandbox)
Group "Premium": premium.monthly and premium.yearly (7-day free trial on yearly only). Open the paywall from any locked section, from Progress, or Settings > Subscription. The billed price is the most prominent; quotas are stated exactly (Free 2/month; Trial up to 10, 5 advanced; Premium 15 advanced per month). Restore Purchases and Redeem Code are in the paywall and in Settings > Subscription. Terms of Use (EULA) and Privacy Policy links are in the paywall, in Settings > Legal and in the App Store description.
After the 2 free analyses, buy the yearly plan in sandbox to continue testing; the trial allows 10 analyses.

AI DISCLOSURE (5.1.2(i))
Feedback text is generated by Claude (Anthropic). All numbers and scores are computed by our own code. Consent is asked just in time, before any data is sent, and can be withdrawn in Settings > Privacy (new analyses are then blocked until the user agrees again). If the filmed person is under 16, no images are uploaded (pose data only) and a guardian attestation is required. Filming someone else requires confirming their consent.

ACCOUNT DELETION (5.1.1(v))
Settings > Delete account, available for anonymous and signed-in users. It deletes all server data, revokes Sign in with Apple, and reminds the user that the Apple subscription must be cancelled separately.

OTHER
- Permissions: camera only (no microphone, no photo-library permission; videos are picked with the system picker).
- iPhone only; tested in iPad compatibility mode.
- No ads, no tracking, no third-party SDKs besides supabase-swift.
- Optional demo account with priority access to the AI service: {{REVIEWER_EMAIL}}. Use "Save history" > Email; the sign-in code is delivered to the mailbox below.
  Mailbox: {{REVIEWER_MAILBOX_URL}} (password in the Sign-In Information field).
- Contact: {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}
```

Character count must stay under 4000 after the placeholders are filled; check it in App Store Connect.

**Sign-In Information fields:** App Store Connect requires them only when sign-in is needed to use the app, which is not the case. Fill them anyway with `{{REVIEWER_EMAIL}}` and the mailbox password so the reviewer can use the flagged account if the shared AI budget is busy.

**Attachment:** for each subscription, attach a paywall screenshot as the review screenshot (DESIGN.md §9.1).
